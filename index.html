Auto reliability

GM technical bulletin targets valvetrain tick: rocker arms and lash adjusters under scrutiny

General Motors’ PIP6101-series bulletins address a top-end ticking noise on recent Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC models, tracing it to debris affecting hydraulic valve lash adjusters. Dealers may inspect and replace all 16 rocker arms and adjusters when defects are found — a service fix, not a formal recall with owner mailings. For owners chasing a persistent “keep ticking” sound, this remains the live shop guidance in 2026.
